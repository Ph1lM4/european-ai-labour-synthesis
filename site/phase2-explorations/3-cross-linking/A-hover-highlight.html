Bidirectional hover-highlight

Hover or focus a country → its class card lights up with the orange ring. Hover or focus a class card → that class’s countries get the orange outline. Always-on, no clicks. On touch, tap acts as hover. Demonstrates the §3 ↔ §4 connection without forcing users to learn a Sankey or click controls.

Try it. Hover any country tile to see its class card highlight. Hover any class card to see its countries highlight on the map.
